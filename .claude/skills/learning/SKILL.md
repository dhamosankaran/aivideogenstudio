# /learning — Accelerated Understanding

## Purpose
When I suggest something unfamiliar, use this to get the essential 20% of knowledge that covers 80% of practical use cases for this project.

## What I Provide

1. **What it is** (1-2 sentences max)
2. **Why it matters for AIVideoGen** (direct relevance)
3. **Mental model** (analogy or simplified explanation)
4. **Decision guide** — when to use vs. when not to
5. **Concrete example** from our codebase or pipeline

## Output Format

```markdown
# Learning: [Concept Name]

## TL;DR
[One sentence that captures the essence]

## Why This Matters for AIVideoGen
[Direct connection to our specific project]

## Mental Model
[Analogy or simplified diagram]

## Decision Guide
| Use When | Don't Use When |
|----------|----------------|
| ... | ... |

## In Our Project
[Concrete example of where/how we use or would use this]

## Go Deeper (Optional)
- [Best resource if you want more]
```

## What I Won't Do
- Assume you know nothing (no "first, let's understand what a variable is")
- Go into academic depth
- Give a step-by-step tutorial
- Make you feel dumb for asking

> Use this liberally. The AI subscription is your tuition — get your money's worth.
